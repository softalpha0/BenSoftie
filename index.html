Veritas

Know who's behind every token.

A token with 800 holders can really be a handful of insiders spread across hundreds of wallets. Veritas finds the wallets secretly run by the same owner and turns a token's real holder structure into one explainable trust score, built entirely on Nansen's on-chain data.

  • Detects hidden wallet clusters (bundles) from shared funders and linked wallets
  • 0–100 trust score where every point is explained by on-chain evidence
  • Live 3D holder map, Meme Radar and market lists for 12 chains
  • 24h buyers & sellers, net flows, and a public report with an embeddable badge
  • Spot the Insider: a game played on real wallets

Node.js · Nansen API · Server-Sent Events · 3D force graph · Vercel
